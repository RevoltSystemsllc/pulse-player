## Proposed notice text

Pulse Digital CMS Player connects a Windows signage computer to a configured
Pulse Digital CMS service. It requires an authorized CMS account and device
enrollment to receive assigned content.

### Information used by the player

To register and operate the device, the player uses device and connection
information such as its hardware identifier, display name, MAC address,
operating-system information, player version, configured CMS address and CMS
authentication information. These values support device identification,
authorization and delivery of assigned content.

The player stores settings, a local content cache and playback state on the
Windows computer. It communicates with the configured CMS to obtain layouts,
media and settings. Depending on configuration and administrator requests, it
can send playback screenshots, diagnostic logs and playback statistics to that
CMS. Screenshots can include the content visible on the signage screen.

### Service and administrator access

The organization operating the configured CMS manages device assignments and
access to uploaded operational information. The final notice must identify
the actual service operator and explain any relevant hosting/processors.
Embedded or externally hosted content may communicate with its own providers;
this draft does not claim that those providers receive no information.

### Storage, retention and requests

Local settings/cache and CMS records have different storage and deletion
behavior. The final notice must explain the operator's real retention and
deletion practices, including how a customer requests removal. Uninstalling
the player is not a promise that all CMS records or external media are deleted.
